# Interview Readiness Guide – AI Infrastructure Performance Engineer

## Purpose
Use this guide to showcase GPU optimisation, profiling, and latency reduction skills sourced from the performance curriculum.

## Core Competencies to Highlight
- GPU profiling, kernel tuning, and model optimisation (quantisation, pruning)  
- Distributed inference performance and capacity planning  
- Cost-performance trade-offs and benchmarking methodology  
- Collaboration with platform and research teams to deliver optimisations

## Preparation Checklist
1. Select performance case studies (GPU profiling, optimisation project, cost reduction).  
2. Collect artefacts: profiling reports, benchmark scripts, before/after metrics.  
3. Practice explaining trade-offs (accuracy vs speed, cost vs throughput).  
4. Prepare narratives about how you partnered with stakeholders to deliver results.

## Mock Interview Structure (50 minutes)
| Segment | Objective | Repo References |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 min | Performance philosophy | Root README |
| 20 min | Technical deep dive (profiling & optimisation) | GPU modules |
| 15 min | Scalability or cost scenario | Distributed inference modules |
| 5 min | Collaboration story | Cross-team notes |
| 5 min | Candidate questions | Below |

## Sample Technical Prompts
- “How do you profile and optimise an LLM serving pipeline on GPUs?”  
- “Describe the trade-offs you consider when choosing a quantisation strategy.”  
- “Walk me through a time you improved throughput without sacrificing reliability.”

## Behavioral Prompts
- Handling conflicting priorities between speed and model accuracy  
- Communicating optimisation outcomes to non-technical stakeholders  
- Iterating on performance fixes under production pressure

## Questions to Ask the Interviewer
- “What performance targets are most critical for your team right now?”  
- “How do you measure the success of performance engineering initiatives?”  
- “What tooling and support does the organisation provide for performance work?”
